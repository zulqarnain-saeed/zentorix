Frequently Asked Questions

What services does ZAQ Technologies offer?
We offer website development, software engineering, mobile apps, UI/UX design, SEO, branding, hosting, and ongoing technical support.

Timelines vary by project scope. A standard website takes 2-4 weeks, while complex software solutions may take 8-16 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during discovery.
Do you offer post-launch support?
Absolutely. All our packages include a support period, and we offer extended maintenance plans for ongoing updates, security patches, and technical assistance.
Can you work with our existing systems?
Yes, we specialize in integrations. Whether you need to connect with existing CRM, ERP, or third-party APIs, our team ensures seamless compatibility.
